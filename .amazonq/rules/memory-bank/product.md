# ChickenLoop - Watersports Job Platform

## Project Overview
ChickenLoop is a specialized full-stack job board platform designed exclusively for the watersports industry. It connects job seekers with recruiters in the marine and watersports sector, providing a tailored experience for this niche market.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Industry-Specific**: Purpose-built for watersports professionals and employers
- **Role-Based Access**: Distinct user experiences for job seekers, recruiters, and administrators
- **Complete Job Lifecycle**: From CV creation to job posting and application management
- **Geographic Integration**: Location-based job matching with interactive mapping

## Key Features

### For Job Seekers
- Create, edit, and manage professional CVs with watersports-specific fields
- Browse comprehensive job listings across the watersports industry
- Privacy-protected CV management (cannot view other users' CVs)
- Location-based job search with interactive maps
- Skills and qualifications tracking for watersports activities

### For Recruiters
- Post and manage job listings with detailed requirements
- Company profile management with location mapping
- Access to qualified candidate pool
- Job posting analytics and management tools
- Industry-specific job categorization

### For Administrators
- Complete user management and oversight
- System-wide data administration
- User role management and permissions
- Platform analytics and monitoring
- Content moderation capabilities

## Target Users

### Primary Users
- **Watersports Professionals**: Instructors, guides, boat operators, marine technicians
- **Watersports Companies**: Dive centers, sailing schools, water sports resorts, marine services
- **Industry Recruiters**: HR professionals specializing in marine and watersports hiring

### Use Cases
- Seasonal employment matching for resort destinations
- Specialized skill-based hiring (diving certifications, sailing qualifications)
- Geographic mobility for traveling watersports professionals
- Career progression within the watersports industry
- Emergency staffing for peak seasons

## Business Model
- B2B platform connecting watersports employers with qualified professionals
- Freemium model with basic job posting and CV creation
- Premium features for enhanced visibility and advanced matching
- Industry-specific value through specialized categorization and requirements
